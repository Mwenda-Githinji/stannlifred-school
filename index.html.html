About St. Annlifred

Founded in 1985, St. Annlifred School has been a beacon of academic excellence and moral upbringing. Our mission is to nurture well-rounded individuals who excel academically, spiritually, and socially.

With state-of-the-art facilities and a dedicated staff, we provide an environment where students can discover and develop their talents.
